Fixed the normalization of the visibilities, the noise and the weights, which did not follow the standard convention of UVFITS files and measurement sets. The visibilities now hold ``V_11 = V_22 = I`` for an unpolarised source by default, the noise of one parallel hand follows the radiometer equation, and the weights are the inverse variance of the visibilities in the file. Before, the UVFITS file had half of the flux, and the noise was too high by a factor of :math:`\sqrt{2}` in Stokes I when ``normalize=True``. Also fixed the FITS files of a bundle overwriting each other in :meth:`~pyvisgen.dataset.SimulateDataSet.from_config` simulations, so that every sample has its own file.
